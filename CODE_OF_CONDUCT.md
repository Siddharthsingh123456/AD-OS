# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, background,
identity, experience level or opinion.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break this code, and may restrict participation.
To report a problem, email the maintainer or use GitHub's private reporting on the repository. Reports are
handled privately and in good faith.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
